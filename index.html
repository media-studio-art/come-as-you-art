COME AS YOU ART

Exhibition

MEDIAART 2B03 - Photo Film IARTS 3BA3 - Artist Book: Narrative / Time MEDIAART 3IO3 - Autobiographical Documentary

Come As You Art brought together students from the Media Arts and iArts Programs who shared stories in ways that go beyond traditional formats.

Across moving images, photography, sound, print, and installation, this exhibition explored how personal experiences can be shaped through both old and new media, careful construction, and hands-on experimentation.
